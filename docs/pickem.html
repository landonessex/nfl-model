    Teach it your pool

    After the deadline, when everyone's picks are visible, paste what share of the pool took each home team. Same format as the slate, with the percentage on the end. This is the highest-value fifteen minutes of your week — it replaces a national average with how your actual 150 people behave.

    Why underdogs

    The public picks favourites more often than favourites win, and the gap widens with the spread. A 3-point favourite wins about 58% of the time and draws about 67% of picks. A 7-point favourite wins about 70% and draws about 85%. That gap is systematic, and it means the contrarian value in a pick'em pool sits almost entirely on underdogs — usually mid-sized ones, around a touchdown, rather than the near coin flips.

    Don't chase last Sunday

    Three underdogs at 40% each miss all three about 22% of the time. That will happen to you four times this season, and it isn't evidence the strategy is wrong. The trap in the other direction is worse: after a contrarian week hits, taking six deviations the next week. The table above already says six is worse than three.
